# DOGGO BATTLES — OPERATION B.A.R.K.

A browser metroidvania. The robo doggos took every doggo but you — because you tore your
chip off, and you're still wearing it, so their doors still open for you.

**Play it in a browser. No install, no build step, no dependencies.** Every file here is
plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; the art is hand-drawn PNG and the music is synthesised
live in WebAudio.

## Chapter 1 — how it works

You do **5 damage**. A robo doggo has **145** and a brute has **175**. That is not a
mistake, and you are not meant to grind it down — **you cannot win a fight.**

Your dash is a *dodge that happens to sting*. You get through the bunker by making the
robots shoot and hit **each other**. A stray laser does 45 to its own side; a brute's
swing does 60.

The robots aren't blind — they've decided you're staff. Walk calmly and they leave you
alone. **Dash, sprint, or crowd them and they work it out**, and the one that does will
shout and turn the whole room. The ceiling lights go from white to red when that happens,
so the room itself tells you your status.

They also check for a friend in the firing line before they take aim — but never again
before they pull the trigger. That gap is the whole game.
